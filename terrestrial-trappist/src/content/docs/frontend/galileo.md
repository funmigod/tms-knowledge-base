## What is Galileo?

Galileo is GoBolt's customer-facing delivery tracking application. It gives customers a real-time view of their delivery, including current status, estimated arrival timing, and important updates throughout the delivery journey.

The application powers the tracking experience shared with customers after a delivery has been scheduled. It serves as the primary interface for communicating delivery progress, driver movement, and key delivery events.

## Who uses Galileo?

Galileo is used by customers receiving GoBolt deliveries. It is designed to answer the questions customers usually have after an order leaves the warehouse: where the delivery is, when it is expected to arrive, and whether anything needs their attention.

## What does Galileo do?

- Shows delivery status and progress
- Displays estimated arrival information
- Communicates important delivery updates
- Provides a customer-friendly tracking experience outside internal operations tools

## How Galileo relates to BVR

BVR is the internal Transportation Management System used by dispatchers and operations teams. Galileo is the external tracking experience customers see. Together, they support the same delivery journey from different sides: BVR helps GoBolt manage the operation, while Galileo keeps the customer informed.
