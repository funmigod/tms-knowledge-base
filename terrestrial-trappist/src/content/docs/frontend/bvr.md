## What is BVR?

BVR is GoBolt's Transportation Management System (TMS). It is the operations-facing web application used by dispatchers and operations teams to plan delivery routes, assign work to drivers, monitor deliveries, and manage day-to-day transportation operations.

The application powers the **Routing**, **Dispatch**, and **Tracking** experiences available at [tms.gobolt.com](http://tms.gobolt.com). It serves as the primary interface for managing last-mile delivery operations.
