## What is Alpine?

Alpine is GoBolt's Delivery Partner Service. It is a backend API responsible for managing delivery partners and the users associated with those partners.

The service supports workflows around delivery partner administration, including partner records, user access, invite flows, and delivery service provider information. It provides the backend interface used by GoBolt systems to manage the partner side of transportation operations.

Alpine is part of the Minipod transportation platform and supports operational workflows where GoBolt needs to coordinate with delivery partners and their users.
