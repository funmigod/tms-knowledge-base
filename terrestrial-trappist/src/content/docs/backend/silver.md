## What is Silver?

Silver is GoBolt's Route Automation Server. It is a backend service responsible for automated routing, telematics integration, and route ETA generation across GoBolt's logistics platform.

The service powers route optimization workflows by generating delivery routes, processing routing data, calculating estimated arrival times, and integrating operational signals from vehicles and delivery networks.

Silver acts as a core transportation intelligence component. It supports dispatch and operations teams by automating route planning decisions and providing accurate routing information to downstream GoBolt systems.
