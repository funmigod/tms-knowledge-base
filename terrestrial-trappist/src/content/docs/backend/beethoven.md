## What is Beethoven?

Beethoven is GoBolt's Route Automation Service and Engine. It is a backend service responsible for executing automated route optimization workflows through integration with Nextmv's route automation engine.

The service acts as a wrapper around Nextmv's routing binary, providing an interface between GoBolt's transportation systems and route optimization algorithms. It enables automated route generation and optimization to support delivery planning and operational efficiency.

Beethoven is a core component of GoBolt's routing infrastructure. It works alongside services such as Silver to automate delivery route planning and improve transportation operations.
