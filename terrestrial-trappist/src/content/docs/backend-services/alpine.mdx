### What is Alpine?

Alpine is GoBolt's Delivery Service Partner Service. It manages delivery partner (DSP) companies and their users — profiles, activation/deactivation, invites, and roles. It's the identity/profile layer for anyone delivering on GoBolt's behalf who isn't a direct GoBolt employee-driver.

---

### Who uses Alpine?

Alpine supports internal systems that manage delivery partners and their users. Core exchanges partner and signup data with Alpine, while BVR uses Alpine for DSP management.

---

### What does Alpine do?

| Area | What Alpine supports |
| --- | --- |
| **Partner profiles** | Stores DSP company profiles and records. |
| **User management** | Manages activation, deactivation, invitations, and roles for partner users. |
| **Delivery-partner identity** | Maintains profiles for people delivering on behalf of GoBolt through a DSP. |
| **Core synchronization** | Exchanges partner, signup, user, invitation, and role data with Core. |


### The Services Connected to Alpine

| Service | How it connects to Alpine |
| --- | --- |
| **Core** | Pushes partner and signup data to Alpine and receives user, invitation, and role-management requests. |
| **BVR** | Uses Alpine's DSP profile and driver-management data. |
