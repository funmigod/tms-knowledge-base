### What is Blue?

Blue is GoBolt's Appointments Service. Despite the name, it isn't customer appointment booking — it owns addresses, delivery zones (geohash-based), and appointment completion data.

---

### Who uses Blue?

Core pushes addresses and completion data into Blue and asks it for zone lookups by postal code; Blue's only call back to Core is validating a user's bearer token. BVR also calls Blue directly for its own appointment and zone-management screens.

---

### What does Blue do?

| Area | What Blue supports |
| --- | --- |
| **Addresses** | Owns address records used by transportation operations. |
| **Delivery zones** | Provides geohash-based delivery-zone lookups by postal code. |
| **Appointment completion data** | Stores appointment completion data rather than customer appointment bookings. |
| **Core synchronization** | Receives address and completion data from Core and serves zone lookups. |


### The Services Connected to Blue

| Service | How it connects to Blue |
| --- | --- |
| **Core** | Sends addresses and completion data, requests zone lookups, and validates bearer tokens used by Blue. |
| **BVR** | Uses Blue for appointment-completion and zone-management experiences. |
