---
title: FBM
description: Fulfilled by Merchant — the merchant warehouses their own goods and GoBolt delivers.
---

**FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant)** is the fulfillment model in which the merchant handles their own warehousing, picking, and packing, and GoBolt provides transportation — typically [last-mile](/glossary/last-mile/) delivery, and sometimes [mid-mile](/glossary/mid-mile/) movement first.

Because GoBolt never holds the inventory, there is no [ASN](/glossary/asn/), no storage, and no pick-and-pack step. The relationship starts when the goods are collected or handed over.

It contrasts with [FBB](/glossary/fbb/), where GoBolt's warehouses hold and fulfil the merchant's stock.
