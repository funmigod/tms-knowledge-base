---
title: Last Mile
description: The final leg of a delivery, from a local facility to the recipient.
---

**Last-mile delivery** is the final leg of the journey: an order moving from a local warehouse, facility, or delivery hub to the recipient's address. It is the stage the customer actually experiences, and the one GoBolt's driver-facing and tracking systems are built around.

Operationally it is the leg that begins after the destination scan (`label_received_at_destination` — *"Package received in local facility"*), where packages are sorted onto routes and loaded into vans, and ends with a [proof of delivery](/glossary/pod/) or a failure that feeds the [RTW](/glossary/rtw/) flow.

It is preceded by [mid-mile](/glossary/mid-mile/) movement, when the goods have to reach the destination market first.
