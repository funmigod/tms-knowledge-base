---
title: GBP
description: GoBolt Parcel, the carrier — and, confusingly, an internal nickname for twin orgs.
---

**GBP (GoBolt Parcel)** means selecting GoBolt itself as the delivery carrier, rather than handing the shipment to a third party such as UPS. It is a carrier choice on a parcel shipment and nothing more: a single-organization mechanism that creates no extra records and makes no call to Hubble.

**These three letters mean something else elsewhere in Core.** The "twin" Transport organization created for [FBB heavy](/glossary/fbb/) merchants is informally nicknamed **"(GBP)"** in a few places — organization display names and an internal dashboard variable. That usage has no relation to GoBolt Parcel the carrier.

The two are worth keeping apart deliberately, because they have been conflated before:

| | GBP the carrier | "(GBP)" the org nickname |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A carrier selection on a shipment | A naming convention for a [twin org](/glossary/twin-org/) |
| Scope | One organization | Two linked organizations |
| Calls Hubble? | No | Yes — Hubble creates the twin order |
| Applies to | Parcel | Heavy |
