---
title: FBB
description: Fulfilled by Bolt — GoBolt stores, picks, and packs the merchant’s inventory.
---

**FBB (Fulfilled by Bolt)** is the fulfillment model in which GoBolt's own warehouses hold a merchant's inventory and pick and pack their orders, as well as delivering them. Inventory arrives by [ASN](/glossary/asn/), is stored, and is drawn down as orders come in.

It contrasts with [FBM](/glossary/fbm/), where the merchant warehouses their own goods and GoBolt provides delivery only.

FBB splits again by package size. Parcel FBB runs through the scan-based flow; **FBB heavy** needs scheduled [appointments](/glossary/appointment/), and it is that combination — heavy goods out of a GoBolt warehouse — that produced the [twin org](/glossary/twin-org/) arrangement and the duplicate-order problem it brings with it.
